# Codex Pulse 图标

终端符号 `>` 表达与 Codex 编程工作的关联；脉冲线表达持续监控任务状态。白瓷圆角底板适配应用的白色风格，蓝至青绿的渐变便于与 Codex 的蓝色云形图标区分。应用、悬浮窗和菜单栏采用同一图标。

- `CodexPulse.png`：设计原图，透明背景。
- `CodexPulse.icns`：macOS 应用图标，包含从 16px 到 1024px 的常用尺寸。
- 生成方式：内置 image_gen 工具；通过系统工具缩放和封装为 ICNS，保留原始图像。

## 最终生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a finished macOS application icon for 'Codex Pulse', a lightweight desktop task-status and account-usage companion to Codex. Generate ONE square 1024x1024 icon, not a presentation or mockup. A refined Apple-style rounded-square app tile, centered with generous 8% transparent margin, genuinely transparent outside the tile. Tile material: soft porcelain white, extremely subtle edge bevel and delicate ambient shadow. In its center a large bold terminal chevron > on the left and a short horizontal pulse waveform on the right (one clean rise and fall, ending in a horizontal baseline), together reading as a distinctive '> pulse' terminal symbol. This echoes the coding/terminal association without copying Codex's blue cloud-shaped logo or the OpenAI knot. The single continuous pulse stroke is the identifying feature for a task monitor. Symbol color gradient: deep cobalt blue on the chevron through cyan to teal on the pulse. Thick, softly rounded, highly legible strokes, restrained, geometrically balanced, premium native macOS utility aesthetic. Symbol takes about 62% of tile width. Use understated dimensional polish, avoid excessive glass/reflections. No letters, no words, no numbers, no badge, no extra decorative elements, no cloud, no OpenAI knot, no checkerboard background. Front-on orthographic view. Deliver a clean transparent PNG icon ready to package into .icns.
